## Editing and verification

The local `fsi-unit1-dialogue-a.mp3` combines seconds 215.8–262.1 of the original recording, including pause practice, with seconds 11.2–27.9 of the continuous dialogue, for a total of 63 seconds. The original audio is preserved without TTS. It was transcoded to a mono 64 kbps MP3. Current timestamps are generated by the local processing worker from its actual recognition result rather than from a preloaded course file.

The Korean text was transcribed from the scanned textbook; spacing was normalized to modern writing, and the response word was transcribed from the recording as `네`. The Chinese text is an Inflow reference translation, not a translation from the original publication. The local Whisper base model was used to help locate audio boundaries and compare them with the textbook; this is not a line-by-line review by a native teacher. Before expanding the material library, the recording should be reviewed again, especially for the old recording quality, honorifics and proper names. The ASR output is not treated as a guaranteed answer key.
